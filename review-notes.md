# What Grok should know about the product: what is intended, who uses it.
# Every line that is not a comment goes into each review.
The views are for technicians (SOC analysts, administrators). Technical terms such as "Standing terms", "Same account seat" or "Seed record" are intended.
Timestamps with milliseconds (for example 01:18:00.000) are intended: analysts need that precision.
A page headline (the h1) stands on one line with nothing beside it on desktop: a headline that wraps while space is free, or with badges or buttons next to it, is a defect.
Tab bars scroll sideways when their tabs do not fit (on phones and tablets): a tab label cut off or faded at the screen edge is intended, not a defect.
The small uppercase line above a page title (for example "PRODUCTION") is the workspace's or organization's name: sample data, not interface text.
