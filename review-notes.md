# What Grok should know about the product: what is intended, who uses it.
# Every line that is not a comment goes into each review.
The views are for technicians (SOC analysts, administrators). Technical terms such as "Standing terms", "Same account seat" or "Seed record" are intended, and so are their German and Spanish translations: do not ask for the English term on a German or Spanish page unless the glossary keeps it.
Timestamps with milliseconds (for example 01:18:00.000) are intended: analysts need that precision.
A page headline (the h1) stands on one line on desktop: a headline that wraps while space is free is a defect. A status badge beside it, and the page's actions at the right of its row, are intended.
Tab bars scroll sideways when their tabs do not fit (on phones and tablets): a tab label cut off or faded at the screen edge is intended, not a defect.
The small uppercase line above a page title (for example "PRODUCTION") is the workspace's or organization's name: sample data, not interface text.
Wide tables scroll sideways under an edge shade, like tab bars: a last column under the shade or at the screen edge is intended.
A report run's title is in the language the report was generated in: an English run may show its English title and contents on a German or Spanish page.
Paired forms for people ("eine Administratorin oder ein Administrator", "als Lesende oder Verantwortliche") are the house style; only role names (Analyst, Betrachter, Betreiber) go without gender forms.
